# Vitals in Orbit (V.I.O)

NASA Space Apps Challenge submission blurb:

Vitals in Orbit (V.I.O) is an offline-first health monitoring tool for astronauts on long-duration missions. It helps crew members track vitals, compare readings to their own baseline, identify early trends, and get actionable guidance for stress, fatigue, sleep, cardiovascular issues, and infection-like patterns. By emphasizing personal baselines, mission hazard context, and clear escalation steps, V.I.O supports astronauts when communication delays make self-assessment critical.

This prototype reflects the NASA Human Research Program focus on behavioral health, cardiovascular performance, bone and muscle preservation, and closed-environment risk mitigation during long-duration exploration missions.
